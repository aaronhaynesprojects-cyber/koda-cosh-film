The Era is drawn, not cut from Occhio's image. Occhio's studio render reflects a white room in its crystal and looks like chrome at night (the V2.2 fault). It is drawn to the proportions of Occhio's own Era grande render, with the lit look taken from Occhio's own film: clear crystal, an opal sphere, bright crystal edges and rim, and a dark base. A publishable master needs Occhio's own night render or cut-out. Ask Occhio for one.

## E. Provenance and permissions
All settings are manufacturer material, none of it cleared for this use:
- DEDON's Spirit of Place renders, from DEDON's press and professional portals.
- Potocco's Izar Houses project photographs, from potocco.it.

Product images come from KODA's own Pablo feed and occhio.com. The Occhio film is used only as a reference for the Era's look.

Captions credit the setting (bottom left) and name the KODA product with "Concept visualisation" (bottom right). The Izar house lighting is the house's own and is never captioned as KODA. Both exports are for internal review only. Neither may be published or sent outside KODA without written permission from DEDON, Potocco, Pablo and Occhio, and Cosh Living's agreement.

## F. Known limits
- Delivered at 1080p. Every framing sits at or under 1:1 against its source, so nothing is upscaled. A 4K master would need the full-size plates for the terrace and Izar close shots, and the Era drawing replaced by Occhio's own image.
- DEDON's material is CGI. The film's only photographs are the Izar shots. Tribù's and Potocco's registered press libraries would add real dusk photography.

## G. Checked before delivery
Frame-by-frame review of the full cut (45 frames, every 2 seconds), against the brief's quality controls. Fixed before render:
- The Izar caption said "Era lusso grande". The lamp drawn is the plain Era grande (clear crystal, no brass mesh), and the caption now says so.
- Changes of framing within one scene (0:34, 0:41, 1:00, 1:03, 1:17) were dissolves, which double-exposed the table into itself. They are now cuts.
- 0:34: reframed on the lantern, sofa end and chair, with no empty floor above.
- 0:41: the fire now sits whole in the frame instead of being cut by its top edge.
- 1:00: pulled back so the table and hall lead and the drawn Era stays secondary.
- 1:03: the Era row sits whole on the left and the Potocco chairs on the right. The near lamp is no longer cut by the frame edge.
