# KODA AT COSH LIVING: Version 2.1 brief

Aaron's notes on Version 2 (21 November 2026):

- Occhio rings floated outdoors, defying gravity.
- The Occhio floor lamp was about 60% oversized and badly placed.
- A Cosh showroom shot had a large sign blocking the view.
- Outdoor settings suit Pablo's Uma or Occhio's new range better.

This brief replaces the Version 2 placement rules.

## The standard
The film should look as if every light was photographed in place. A lighting designer or architect watching it should find nothing that couldn't exist.

## Rules
1. **Gravity.** Every KODA piece visibly stands on a surface or hangs from structure in frame. No pendants outdoors.
2. **Scale from data.** Size each piece from the manufacturer's published dimensions, measured against furniture in the same plane of the image (dining table 74–75 cm, coffee table 35–40 cm, sofa seat 40–45 cm). Never use a yardstick taken from another depth.
   - Occhio Era: ø 16.4 × H 16.8 cm
   - Occhio Era grande: ø 20.9 × H 25 cm
   - Pablo Uma: ø 15.4 × H 28 cm, 34 cm with strap
3. **Outdoors, cordless only.**
   - Occhio Era lusso: Occhio places it "at the dining table, on the sideboard, or on the patio"; 8 hours per charge.
   - Pablo Uma: indoor and outdoor, IP50, 190 lm, 2000–2700 K warm-dim.
4. **Swap, don't add, where a scene already has a lamp.** The KODA piece takes the lamp's exact place, so the render's own light becomes KODA's light. The original lamp is removed from the plate, not hidden.
5. **Light behaves like the product.**
   - Era lusso: a bright core, sparkle on the glass, a pool on the surface below.
   - Uma: a soft, even, warm glow and a low pool around its base.
   - Unlit pieces sit at the scene's own brightness: no studio highlights in a dusk frame.
6. **Restraint.** One lighting idea per scene, one to three pieces.
7. **Cosh Living shown clean.** No signage, price tickets or display cards in frame; remove a ticket rather than crop round it.
8. **Occhio's new range in the brand section.** Era 4K campaign footage replaces the Mito largo stills.
