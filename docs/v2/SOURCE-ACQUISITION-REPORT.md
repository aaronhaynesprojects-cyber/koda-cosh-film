## Tribù (priority 2): no media library without registration

- **Press lounge** (tribu.com/en/press-lounge): "high-res images and videos are in our download area upon registration". Contact: Sara Ceyssens, press@tribu.com, +32 497 63 84 40. Not registered: that is Aaron's or KODA's to do.
- **Public project pages** (24 projects, 103 photographs) serve their files from Tribù's image server, some at full size (up to 7,605 px).
- **Ownership.** These are hotel and residence projects (Four Seasons, St Regis, Abadia Retuerta, Villa Geba and others). The photography typically belongs to the property or photographer, for example Michel Figuet at Villa Geba.
