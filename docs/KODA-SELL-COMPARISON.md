# Selling KODA: first three reels vs the best-of (#1)

22 November 2026. Reels compared from the assembly: V1 narrated film (0:02–1:32), KODA brands B-roll (1:34–2:36), Occhio Era C-roll (2:38–3:15), against `v=best` with Rocket Jr and Marie.

## What sold KODA in the first three reels

1. **KODA itself was on screen.** V1 0:18–0:26 is KODA's own Lightspace showroom film: the KODA mark on the glass at night, then the full-screen KODA mark (0:23–0:26). The B-roll opens on the same shot. The best-of never shows KODA until the joint end card at 1:24.
2. **Every lighting reel signed off as KODA.** The B-roll and C-roll both end on the KODA mark alone, which says "these brands are KODA". In the best-of, the lighting brands read as Occhio, Pablo and the rest, with no owner.
3. **The narration named the portfolio, brand by brand.** In V1, each line sits on its own brand, with its country caption: "A world of extraordinary ideas" over Occhio, "sculpted in glass" over A-N-D, "drawn in shadow" over Lodes, "defined by the smallest detail" over Pablo, and "Light that … transforms it" over Arkoslight. That reads as the range of one house: five makers from five countries.
4. **The products were heroes in rooms.** V1's roll-call showed the statement pieces large in furnished interiors: A-N-D columns, the Lodes Random cluster, the Pablo pendants, Arkoslight rooms. The best-of's lighting section (0:56–1:13) is mostly macro and abstract detail.
