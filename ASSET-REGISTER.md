## Concept visualisations (composites)

Each starts as the genuine showroom photograph. The KODA fixture then fades in, and the frame carries the label "Concept visualisation" from that moment. Nothing in the showroom itself is altered: no furniture, finish or architecture is moved or redrawn.

Scale was set from the furniture in each photograph (table diameters and sofa length estimated by eye, not measured). **Sizes and hanging heights are indicative** and should be checked against the real showroom dimensions and the manufacturers' data sheets before anyone treats these frames as a design.
